# Acceptable Use Policy · 合法使用条款

This policy applies to everyone who uses the CellPilot app, the CellPilot push relay, or the
specification, schemas, guide and tools in this repository. Each section is given in English,
then in Chinese.

本政策适用于所有使用 CellPilot App、CellPilot 推送中转，或本仓库中的规范、schema、指南和工具的人。每一节先写英文，再写中文。

## Purpose · 宗旨

CellPilot is designed so that people whose iPhone takes only eSIM do not miss the texts and calls
of a physical SIM card of their own, in a cellular module they control. It is made mainly for
receiving: calling and texting are there for the occasional need and are strictly limited, and a
number used every day belongs in a phone or on an eSIM. It is made only for lawful purposes. We abide by the law and do not endorse, support
or assist any unlawful activity. If you find a problem — misuse of CellPilot, or anything in the
product or these documents that conflicts with laws or regulations — please tell us right away at
**abuse@cellpilot.dev**. We will cooperate with the authorities and rectify it, and if necessary
change, restrict or shut down the feature or service concerned.

CellPilot 的设计初衷，是让只能使用 eSIM 的 iPhone 用户，不错过插在自己掌控的蜂窝模块里、自己另一张实体 SIM 卡上的短信和来电。它以接收为主：拨号和发短信只是为偶尔的必要情况提供的便利，有严格的次数限制；日常使用的号码应当放进手机或转为 eSIM。它仅为合法用途而制作。我们遵守法律法规，不认可、不支持、也不协助任何违法活动。如果你发现问题——CellPilot 被滥用，或产品、本文档中有任何与法律法规相抵触的内容——请立即通过 **abuse@cellpilot.dev** 告诉我们。我们会配合有关部门并予以整改，必要时变更、限制或关闭相关功能或服务。

## You may · 你可以

With CellPilot you may:

- Use a SIM card that is your own and, where the law requires it, registered under your real name.
- Use it from your own devices: your own backend, module and iPhones.
- Build, run and share backends that follow the specification and this policy.

使用 CellPilot，你可以：

- 使用属于你自己的 SIM 卡；法律要求实名登记的，该卡须以你本人的真实身份登记。
- 在你自己的设备上使用它：你自己的后端、模块和 iPhone。
- 编写、运行和分享遵循本规范和本政策的后端。

## You must not · 你不得

You must not:

- Use CellPilot for fraud, scams, harassment, or any other unlawful activity.
- Rent, lend, sell or share SIM cards or lines, or use a SIM card that belongs to someone else.
- Make bulk, automated or unsolicited calls or send bulk, automated or unsolicited messages.
- Receive or forward verification codes for other people, or run a code-receiving service.
- Change, improperly hide or spoof the calling number, or route other people's calls or messages
  through your line.
- Use more than one SIM card per backend, rotate SIM cards, or build SIM or module pools.
- Evade or test carriers' risk controls, or circumvent network management.
- Use cellular modules that lack the type approval or network-access licence required where you
  use them.
- Circumvent the limits on calls and texts, in the app or in a backend, or build a backend
  without them to get around them.

你不得：

- 将 CellPilot 用于诈骗、欺诈、骚扰或任何其他违法活动。
- 出租、出借、出售或与他人共用 SIM 卡或线路，或使用属于他人的 SIM 卡。
- 拨打或发送批量、自动化或未经请求的电话或短信。
- 替他人接收或转发验证码，或经营接码服务。
- 更改、不当隐藏或伪造主叫号码，或让他人的电话、短信经你的线路中转。
- 一个后端使用多张 SIM 卡、轮换 SIM 卡，或搭建 SIM 卡池或模块池。
- 规避或试探运营商的风控措施，或绕过网络管理。
- 使用在使用地未取得所需型号核准或进网许可的蜂窝模块。
- 规避 App 或后端中对拨号和短信的次数限制，或为绕过这些限制而编写不带限制的后端。

## Your responsibility · 你的责任

You are responsible for following the telecommunications, recording and privacy laws that apply
where you, your SIM card and the people you call or message are, and your carrier's terms. Some
carriers do not allow their SIM cards in gateway devices or modules; check before you use one.

你有责任遵守你本人、你的 SIM 卡以及与你通话或通信的人所在地适用的电信、录音和隐私法律法规，以及你的运营商的服务条款。有些运营商不允许将其 SIM 卡用于网关设备或模块；使用前请先确认。

## Emergency calls · 紧急呼叫

CellPilot is not a replacement for phone service and must not be relied on for emergency calls.
A call depends on your Internet connection, your backend, the module and the carrier, any of which
can fail. An emergency call made through CellPilot leaves from the module's location, not yours:
it may reach the wrong emergency centre, or one in another country or region, which then sees the
wrong location. In an emergency, use a regular phone.

CellPilot 不能替代电话服务，不能依赖它拨打紧急电话。通话依赖你的互联网连接、后端、模块和运营商，其中任何一环都可能出故障。经 CellPilot 拨出的紧急呼叫从模块所在地发出，而不是从你所在的地方：它可能接到错误的急救中心，甚至另一个国家或地区的急救中心，对方看到的也是错误的位置。遇到紧急情况，请使用普通电话。

## Recording · 录音

The answering machine, call screening and transcription record and transcribe callers. Depending
on where the caller and you are, the law may require telling the caller or getting their consent.
Whoever runs the backend is responsible for complying with those laws, for example by playing a
greeting that says the call is answered by a machine and recorded.

答录机、来电筛选和转写会对来电者录音并转写。视来电者和你所在地的法律，可能需要告知来电者或取得其同意。后端的运行者负责遵守这些法律，例如播放问候语，说明来电由答录机接听并会被录音。

## Reporting and our response · 举报与我们的处理

Report misuse, or anything in CellPilot or these documents that conflicts with laws or
regulations, to **abuse@cellpilot.dev**. We will cooperate with the authorities and rectify the
problem, and if necessary change, restrict or shut down the feature or service concerned.

The push relay is currently invitation-only and offered on terms given to invitees. We may limit,
suspend or close relay accounts that break this policy, and we cooperate with lawful requests from
the authorities. Security vulnerabilities go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

滥用行为，或 CellPilot、本文档中任何与法律法规相抵触的内容，请报告至 **abuse@cellpilot.dev**。我们会配合有关部门并予以整改，必要时变更、限制或关闭相关功能或服务。

推送中转目前仅限受邀使用，按提供给受邀者的条款提供。对违反本政策的中转账号，我们可以限制、暂停或关闭，并配合有关部门依法提出的要求。安全漏洞请按 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 报告。
