CellPilot Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

English · 简体中文

CellPilot is an iPhone app that works with a backend you run yourself, next to your own cellular module and SIM card. This policy explains what the app and the CellPilot push relay handle, and what we, the developer of CellPilot, can and cannot see.

Your messages, calls and contacts

Your SMS messages, call history, voicemails, transcripts and contacts live on your backend and on your iPhone. The app talks to your backend directly — on your network, or through the remote access you set up — and none of this content passes through or is stored on any server of ours. We never receive it.

On your iPhone

  • The pairing token and the addresses of your backend are kept in the iOS Keychain. The app keeps a copy of your conversations, calls and voicemails on the device so it opens quickly. Unpairing removes them.
  • Microphone: used only during calls, to carry your voice to your backend.
  • Contacts: read or written only when you choose to import or export contacts.
  • Local network: used to find and reach your backend on your own network.

The push relay (optional)

To wake your iPhone when the app is closed, your backend sends notifications through the CellPilot push relay, which passes them to Apple Push Notification service. Using it requires Sign in with Apple.

  • What the relay keeps: Apple's identifier for your account (we ask for no name and no email), your devices' push tokens, the backends you enrolled (an identifier, a public key and a label), the invite you redeemed, and daily counts of the notifications sent.
  • What it cannot read: the content of each notification is encrypted on your backend with a key only your iPhone and your backend hold. A backend that follows the CellPilot Device API sends nothing readable to the relay but a short generic title (such as “New message”) and the badge count.
  • What it sees to deliver: when each notification is sent, to which device, and the network address of the backend sending it.

Share Usage Data

If Share Usage Data is on (Settings › Diagnostics), the app sends its own log to the relay so we can find and fix problems: what the app did and when, errors, crash and hang reports, the app and iOS versions, the iPhone model and a random identifier for this installation. The log never contains message text, phone numbers or contacts. It is on by default in TestFlight beta builds and off by default otherwise; you can turn it off at any time. Logs are kept for 30 days.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no third-party analytics, and we do not sell or share your data. Data is used only to run the relay and to improve CellPilot.

Where it is processed

The relay runs on Cloudflare's network, which processes network addresses to deliver requests. Notifications are delivered by Apple Push Notification service.

Deleting your data

In the app's Settings, Delete Account (shown once you are signed in to the push relay) deletes your relay account and everything the relay keeps for it — push tokens, enrolled backends, usage counts and logs — at once. Unpairing removes what the app keeps on your iPhone. Your backend's data is under your own control.

Children

CellPilot is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: privacy@cellpilot.dev.

CellPilot 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 3 日

English · 简体中文

CellPilot 是一个 iPhone App，配合你自己运行的后端使用，后端接着你自己的蜂窝模块和 SIM 卡。本政策说明 App 和 CellPilot 推送中转处理哪些数据，以及我们作为 CellPilot 的开发者能看到什么、看不到什么。

你的短信、通话和联系人

你的短信、通话记录、留言、转写和联系人保存在你的后端和你的 iPhone 上。App 直接连接你的后端——在你的 网络里，或经你自己设置的远程访问——这些内容不经过、也不保存在我们的任何服务器上。我们收不到它们。

在你的 iPhone 上

  • 配对令牌和后端地址保存在 iOS 钥匙串里。App 在本机保存会话、通话和留言的副本，以便快速打开。取消配对会删除它们。
  • 麦克风：只在通话时使用，把你的声音传给你的后端。
  • 通讯录：只在你选择导入或导出联系人时读写。
  • 本地网络：用于在你自己的网络里找到并连接你的后端。

推送中转（可选）

为了在 App 关着时唤醒你的 iPhone，你的后端通过 CellPilot 推送中转发送通知，再由中转交给 Apple 推送 通知服务。使用中转需要"通过 Apple 登录"。

  • 中转保存的：Apple 给你账号的标识（我们不索取姓名和邮箱）、你设备的推送 token、你登记的后端 （一个标识、一把公钥和一个名称）、你兑换的邀请码，以及每天发送通知的数量。
  • 中转读不到的：每条通知的内容都在你的后端用只有你的 iPhone 和你的后端持有的密钥加密。遵循 CellPilot 设备 API 的后端，发给中转的可读内容只有一个简短的通用标题（如"新消息"）和角标数。
  • 为了投递而能看到的：每条通知的发送时间、发往哪台设备，以及发送它的后端的网络地址。

共享使用数据

打开共享使用数据（设置 › 诊断）时，App 会把自己的日志发给中转，帮助我们发现和修复问题：App 做了什么、什么时候、出了什么错、崩溃和卡顿报告、App 和 iOS 版本、iPhone 型号，以及这次安装的随机标识。 日志从不包含短信内容、电话号码或联系人。TestFlight 测试版默认打开，其他版本默认关闭，你随时可以关掉。 日志保存 30 天。

我们不做的事

没有广告，不跨 App 或网站追踪你，不使用第三方统计，也不出售或分享你的数据。数据只用于运行中转和改进 CellPilot。

在哪里处理

中转运行在 Cloudflare 的网络上，为了送达请求会处理网络地址。通知由 Apple 推送通知服务投递。

删除你的数据

在 App 的设置里点删除账号（登录推送中转后才会出现），会立即删除你的中转账号和中转为它保存的一切——推送 token、 登记的后端、使用计数和日志。取消配对会删除 App 在 iPhone 上保存的内容。你后端里的数据由你自己掌握。

儿童

CellPilot 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，新版本会发布在本页并更新生效日期。有问题请联系： privacy@cellpilot.dev。
