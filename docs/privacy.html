CellPilot Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

English · 简体中文

CellPilot is an iPhone app that works with a backend you run yourself, next to your own cellular module and SIM card. This policy explains what the app and the CellPilot push relay handle, and what we, the developer of CellPilot, can and cannot see.

Your messages, calls and contacts

Your SMS messages, call history, voicemails, transcripts and contacts live on your backend and on your iPhone. Calls and messages travel directly between the app and your backend — on your network, or through the remote access you set up — and no server of ours carries them or stores them. If you turn on push notifications, a notification about a new message, call or voicemail passes through the push relay end-to-end encrypted, which the relay is designed to be unable to decrypt (see below).

On your iPhone

  • The pairing token and the addresses of your backend are kept in the iOS Keychain. The app keeps a copy of your conversations, calls and voicemails on the device so it opens quickly. Unpairing removes them.
  • Microphone: used only during calls, to carry your voice to your backend.
  • Contacts: read or written only when you choose to import or export contacts.
  • Local network: used to find and reach your backend on your own network.

Copies kept by iOS

Some data also reaches parts of iOS that Apple controls, not us: calls shown through the system call screen appear in the Phone app's Recents; contacts you export are saved in Contacts; either may be synced to iCloud under Apple's privacy policy if you use iCloud; and notifications may be shown on the lock screen, depending on your notification settings. Removing CellPilot does not remove these copies; manage them in iOS.

The push relay (optional)

To wake your iPhone when the app is closed, your backend sends notifications through the CellPilot push relay, which passes them to Apple Push Notification service. The relay is currently available by invitation only, on terms given to invitees. Using it requires Sign in with Apple.

  • What the relay keeps: Apple's identifier for your account (we ask for no name and no email), your devices' push tokens, the backends you enrolled (an identifier, a public key and a label), the invite you redeemed, and daily counts of the notifications sent.
  • What it cannot read: when your backend follows the CellPilot Device API, the content of each notification is encrypted on your backend with a key only your iPhone and your backend hold, so neither the relay nor Apple can read it. Such a backend sends nothing readable to the relay but a short generic title (such as “New message”) and the badge count.
  • What it sees to deliver: when each notification is sent; to which device; what kind it is (message, call, voicemail, security alert or badge) and its reference (such as sms-42); the generic title and the badge count, which travel in the clear; the label of the backend sending it; the network addresses of the backend, and of your iPhone when it contacts the relay; and Apple's delivery result.

Share Usage Data

If Share Usage Data is on (Settings › Diagnostics), the app sends its own log to the relay so we can find and fix problems: what the app did and when, errors, crash and hang reports, the app and iOS versions, the iPhone model and a random identifier for this installation. The log is designed to exclude message text, phone numbers, contacts and device identifiers such as IMEI, IMSI and ICCID. When logs are sent, the relay also sees your iPhone's network address. The app asks before turning it on (in TestFlight builds it asks at first launch; otherwise it is off until you turn it on), and you can turn it off at any time. Logs are kept for 30 days.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show advertising, track you across apps or websites, or use third-party analytics. We share data only with the providers that run the relay (Cloudflare) and deliver notifications (Apple), and when the law requires it. Data is used only to run the relay and to improve CellPilot.

Where it is processed

The relay runs on Cloudflare's global network, which processes network addresses to deliver requests. Notifications are delivered by Apple Push Notification service. Your data may therefore be processed outside your country or region, including in the United States.

Deleting your data

In the app's Settings, Delete Account (shown once you are signed in to the push relay) deletes your relay account and everything the relay keeps for it — push tokens, enrolled backends, usage counts and logs — promptly; copies in our providers' short-lived logs and backups expire on their own. Unpairing removes what the app keeps on your iPhone. Your backend's data is under your own control.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to receive a copy of it, to withdraw consent, to object to its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Most of this can be done in the app, with Delete Account and Share Usage Data; otherwise write to privacy@cellpilot.dev.

Requests from authorities

We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it. We cannot provide the content of your messages or calls, because we do not have it.

Lawful use

CellPilot is made only for lawful purposes. We abide by the law and do not endorse, support or assist any unlawful activity. If you find misuse, or anything in CellPilot that conflicts with laws or regulations, please tell us right away at abuse@cellpilot.dev. We will cooperate with the authorities and rectify it, and if necessary change, restrict or shut down the feature or service concerned.

Children

CellPilot is not directed at children under 14.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: privacy@cellpilot.dev.

CellPilot 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日

English · 简体中文

CellPilot 是一个 iPhone App，配合你自己运行的后端使用，后端接着你自己的蜂窝模块和 SIM 卡。本政策说明 App 和 CellPilot 推送中转处理哪些数据，以及我们作为 CellPilot 的开发者能看到什么、看不到什么。

你的短信、通话和联系人

你的短信、通话记录、留言、转写和联系人保存在你的后端和你的 iPhone 上。通话和短信在 App 与你的后端之间直接传输——在你的 网络里，或经你自己设置的远程访问——不经由我们的任何服务器承载，也不保存在我们的服务器上。如果你开启推送通知，关于新短信、来电或留言的通知会经过推送中转， 它是端到端加密的，中转按设计无法解密（见下文）。

在你的 iPhone 上

  • 配对令牌和后端地址保存在 iOS 钥匙串里。App 在本机保存会话、通话和留言的副本，以便快速打开。取消配对会删除它们。
  • 麦克风：只在通话时使用，把你的声音传给你的后端。
  • 通讯录：只在你选择导入或导出联系人时读写。
  • 本地网络：用于在你自己的网络里找到并连接你的后端。

iOS 保存的副本

有些数据还会进入由 Apple 而不是我们掌控的 iOS 部分：经系统来电界面显示的通话会出现在"电话"App 的"最近通话"里；你导出的联系人保存在"通讯录"里； 如果你使用 iCloud，二者都可能按 Apple 的隐私政策同步到 iCloud；通知可能显示在锁定屏幕上，取决于你的通知设置。删除 CellPilot 不会删除这些副本，请在 iOS 中管理它们。

推送中转（可选）

为了在 App 关着时唤醒你的 iPhone，你的后端通过 CellPilot 推送中转发送通知，再由中转交给 Apple 推送 通知服务。中转目前仅限受邀使用，按提供给受邀者的条款提供。使用中转需要"通过 Apple 登录"。

  • 中转保存的：Apple 给你账号的标识（我们不索取姓名和邮箱）、你设备的推送 token、你登记的后端 （一个标识、一把公钥和一个名称）、你兑换的邀请码，以及每天发送通知的数量。
  • 中转读不到的：你的后端遵循 CellPilot 设备 API 时，每条通知的内容都在你的后端用只有你的 iPhone 和你的后端持有的密钥加密， 中转和 Apple 都读不到。这样的后端发给中转的可读内容只有一个简短的通用标题（如"新消息"）和角标数。
  • 为了投递而能看到的：每条通知的发送时间；发往哪台设备；它的类型（短信、来电、留言、安全告警或角标）和引用（如 sms-42）；以明文传递的通用标题和角标数；发送它的后端的名称；后端的网络地址，以及你的 iPhone 联系中转时的网络地址； 以及 Apple 返回的投递结果。

共享使用数据

打开共享使用数据（设置 › 诊断）时，App 会把自己的日志发给中转，帮助我们发现和修复问题：App 做了什么、什么时候、出了什么错、崩溃和卡顿报告、App 和 iOS 版本、iPhone 型号，以及这次安装的随机标识。 日志按设计不包含短信内容、电话号码、联系人，以及 IMEI、IMSI、ICCID 等设备标识。发送日志时，中转还能看到你 iPhone 的网络地址。 App 会先征得你同意再打开它（TestFlight 测试版在首次启动时询问；其他版本在你自己打开之前一直关闭），你随时可以关掉。日志保存 30 天。

我们不做的事

我们不出售你的数据，不展示广告，不跨 App 或网站追踪你，也不使用第三方统计。我们只与运行中转的服务商（Cloudflare）和投递通知的服务商（Apple） 共享数据，以及在法律要求时提供。数据只用于运行中转和改进 CellPilot。

在哪里处理

中转运行在 Cloudflare 的全球网络上，为了送达请求会处理网络地址。通知由 Apple 推送通知服务投递。因此你的数据可能在你所在的国家或地区以外处理，包括美国。

删除你的数据

在 App 的设置里点删除账号（登录推送中转后才会出现），会及时删除你的中转账号和中转为它保存的一切——推送 token、 登记的后端、使用计数和日志；服务商短期日志和备份中的副本会自行过期。取消配对会删除 App 在 iPhone 上保存的内容。你后端里的数据由你自己掌握。

你的权利

视你所在地的法律，你可能有权访问、更正或删除你的数据，获取数据副本，撤回同意，反对对数据的处理，以及向数据保护（个人信息保护）主管部门投诉。 其中大部分可以在 App 里通过删除账号和共享使用数据完成；其他请写信至 privacy@cellpilot.dev。

有关部门的要求

我们只在法律要求时向有关部门披露数据。我们无法提供你的短信或通话内容，因为我们没有这些内容。

合法使用

CellPilot 仅为合法用途而制作。我们遵守法律法规，不认可、不支持、也不协助任何违法活动。如果你发现滥用，或 CellPilot 中有任何与法律法规相抵触的内容， 请立即通过 abuse@cellpilot.dev 告诉我们。我们会配合有关部门并予以整改，必要时变更、限制或关闭相关功能或服务。

儿童

CellPilot 不面向不满 14 周岁的儿童。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，新版本会发布在本页并更新生效日期。有问题请联系： privacy@cellpilot.dev。
